How it works, in order: 1) Use a personal wallet on BNB Chain. 2) Call mint(quantity) — quantity 1 or 2. 3) Send exactly the fee for those tokens: 0 BNB for every token numbered 1–200, 0.00025 BNB for every token numbered 201–10,000. A quantity that crosses 200 pays 0 for the free part and 0.00025 for each paid part. The contract checks the value and rejects any other amount. The fee is paid to the contract; the owner withdraws it with withdraw(). Network gas is separate.

Article V · FAQ

    
Questions

    

      
How many?
10,000. The first 200 are FREE; from 201 onward each take costs a 0.00025 BNB fee paid to the contract, plus gas.

      
How many per wallet?
Max 2 takes per wallet, in total — not per transaction.

      
How do I mint?
Tap CONNECT WALLET then MINT on this page (MetaMask, Trust, OKX or any BNB Chain wallet), or copy the agent prompt into an AI agent wallet.

      
What if my mint crosses 200?
Example: if 199 are minted and you mint 2, you pay 0 for #200 and 0.00025 BNB for #201 — 0.00025 BNB in total. The contract calculates it with priceFor(quantity).

      
Why did my mint fail?
Usually one of four: the value sent was wrong, the 2-per-wallet limit was reached, the collection sold out, or mint is not open yet.

      
What about the old contract?
The old contract (0xa69a…d60f, 120 supply, 0.0002 / 0.002 BNB) is superseded by v2 for this site. It still exists on-chain, but this site no longer mints on it once v2 is live.

      
What's the link to RiferaScreen?
Every First Take is a cursor from the RiferaScreen recorder, the app that zooms, frames and polishes every take automatically.

      
Will it go up in value?
No promises. It is collectible pixel art, not an investment.
