No. 06 — FAQ

Questions

How many?

3,333: 1,111 at 0.0002 BNB and 2,222 at $1.50. Fusion burns 3 and mints 1, so supply shrinks.

Why no connect-wallet?

Minting is agent-only. Copy a prompt or the manual parameters into an agent that can send a BNB Chain transaction.

What does the cheap mint cost?

0.0002 BNB per NFT plus gas, max 7 per wallet.

How does the $1.50 mint work?

$1.50 of BNB per NFT, max 20 per wallet. The price follows Chainlink BNB/USD on-chain; your agent reads priceFor(N) and sends that value. Overpay is refunded.

Why did my mint fail?

Usually one of three: the value sent was wrong, the per-wallet limit was reached, or the three takes in a fusion are not the same tier.

What's the link to RiferaScreen?

Every First Take is a cursor from the RiferaScreen recorder, the app that zooms, frames and polishes every take automatically.

Will it go up in value?

No promises. It is collectible pixel art, not an investment.
