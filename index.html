No. 06 — FAQ

Questions

How many?

120: 40 at 0.0002 BNB and 80 at 0.002 BNB.

How do I mint?

Tap MINT WITH WALLET (MetaMask, Trust, OKX or any BNB Chain wallet), or copy the agent prompt into an AI agent wallet.

What does the cheap mint cost?

0.0002 BNB per NFT plus gas, max 7 per wallet.

How does the 0.002 BNB mint work?

0.002 BNB per NFT, fixed, max 20 per wallet. Your agent sends 0.002 BNB × N with mintPaid(N), or reads priceFor(N). Overpay is refunded.

Why did my mint fail?

Usually one of three: the value sent was wrong, the per-wallet limit was reached, the column sold out, or mint is not open yet.

What's the link to RiferaScreen?

Every First Take is a cursor from the RiferaScreen recorder, the app that zooms, frames and polishes every take automatically.

Will it go up in value?

No promises. It is collectible pixel art, not an investment.
